# 18 — A near miss shields the abandoned attempt standing next to it

**What to build:** A run with nothing of the line opposite it removed even when the
matched word beside it is a near miss. `stumbles` refuses the removal whenever either
neighbour was matched as a mishearing, and on `Sequence 07` line 1 that neighbour is
`coating` for `coding` — so seven words of an abandoned attempt play inside the take.

**Blocked by:** None

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Listen for

1. `Sequence 07` line 1 at `02:24` — `oh no white coating one no no` does not play. The
   reading is `…with a wipe coating / part two` and nothing between.
2. `sequence.mp4` line 1 at `00:00` — `the wipe` is still in the take and still audible.
   That is the pair the guard was written for and it must go on holding.
3. Both recordings, every line — no line loses a word of its own. This rule removes, so
   a guard loosened too far deletes speech nothing records but the report.

- [ ] A run is shielded only where the near miss beside it is the other half of one
      mishearing — the transcriber writing two words where the line has one
- [ ] Not where the near miss is a whole word of the line heard slightly wrong, with the
      run standing apart from it
- [ ] Decision 0002 still holds: the matched near miss itself is never removed
- [ ] Tests: `the wipe` stays; a run beside a lone near miss goes

## Comments

Found while fixing the run's other end (decision 0004, amended 31 August): the take now
reaches `part two` across the abandoned attempt instead of ending before it, so the
attempt is `stumbles`' business rather than an off-script region's. It played either
way; what changed is which rule is in a position to remove it.

`_beside_a_near_miss` asks only whether the token either side was matched to a word it
does not equal. Both cases answer yes. What separates them is whether the script word
opposite the run was taken by the near miss — `vibe` standing opposite both `the` and
`wipe` — or whether the line simply has nothing there, which is the case here: `coating`
holds `coding` on its own and `oh no white coating one no no` is opposite nothing at all.
